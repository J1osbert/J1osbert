FILM-MAKING TERMS

ARC SHOT
INTERLUDE
PROLOGUE

ARC SHOT

A SHOT IN WHICH THE SUBJECT IS PHOTHRAPHED BY AN ENCIRCLING OR MOVING CAMERA

INTERLUDE

A BRIEF, INTERVENING FIL SCENE OR SEQUENCE, NOT SPECIFICALLY TIED TO THE PLOT, THAT APPEARS WITHIN A FILM

PROLOGUE

A SPEECH, PREFACE, INTRODUCTION OR BRIEF SCENE PRECEEDING THE MAIN ACTION OR PLOT OF A FILM; contrast to epilogue
