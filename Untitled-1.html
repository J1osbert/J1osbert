THE CHARADES OF LIFE

LIFE LIKE THERE IS NO OTHER

MY BELOVED TORMENTOR,
PLEASE STAY A LITTLE LONGER.
I SEE MYSELF IN YOUR EYES.
FOR IN YOU MY ONLY DESIRE RELIES.
RAIN DROPS GENTLY KISS YOUR FACE.
HOW I WISH YOUR LIPS WITH MINE WILL LACE.
I HAVE DREAMT QUITE A WHILE NOW,
BUT MY SURE DESTINATION IS IN YOUR ARMS.
